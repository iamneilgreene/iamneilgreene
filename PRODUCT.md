## Register

brand

The site is the digital headquarters of a personal brand. Design IS the product.
The one genuine product surface inside it is the Four M Capability Profile
assessment at `/capability-profile/start`, which is treated as a product
register locally: quiet, focused, distraction-light.

## Users

**Primary.** Builders, leaders, and people carrying meaningful responsibility
who want to expand what they can understand, access, handle, and lead. Broadly
30 to 50: professionals, executives, entrepreneurs, parents, leaders who are
already successful in at least one area and quietly fragile in another.

They arrive from social, search, or referral, usually on a phone, usually
skeptical, usually after seeing a short video. They have seen a hundred coaching
sites and can smell one instantly. The job to be done: find out where their own
capability is actually thin, and get one thing to do about it.

**Secondary.** Organisations and event bookers evaluating Neil for advisory,
AI/cybersecurity work, or speaking. They need senior credibility fast and will
not read a manifesto to find it.

**Audience architecture (locked).** The umbrella brand is not men-only.
Capability, the Four Dimensions, the Profile, Ideas, speaking, and advisory are
open to everyone. Men-only language is reserved for the brotherhood and
explicitly male-focused programs. Never append "for men" to general capability
language.

## Product Purpose

Convert attention into an owned relationship through the Four M Capability
Profile, and establish Neil Greene as the authority behind the Capability
worldview.

Six jobs, in order:

1. Make the Capability idea memorable in the first 20 to 30 seconds.
2. Convert cold and warm traffic into email via the Profile.
3. Prove range through lived evidence, not a résumé.
4. Route high-intent visitors into the right commercial path without cluttering
   the main narrative.
5. Give followers a reason to return.
6. Bridge the online audience to a real-world community.

Success is not traffic. Success is Profile completions, email capture, and
people who come back in 75 days to retest.

## Brand Personality

**Three words: calm, exact, earned.**

Archetype mix (locked): Sage 40%, Ruler 30%, Hero 20%, Jester 10%. An applied
Sage, not a detached professor. Studies in order to use and teach.

Voice speaks from one of three honest positions: "I know this," "I experienced
this," "I am studying this." Never pretends to be the world's greatest expert in
every subject.

Emotional goal: the visitor should feel measured, not motivated. Respected, not
sold. The market gap being occupied is **calm power**: intelligent, modern
masculinity without chest-beating.

Non-negotiable language:

- Responsibility demands capability.
- Expand capability. Carry responsibility.
- Mind, Means, Measure, Mastery. What do you know / have / can you handle / can
  you lead.
- Get Your Capability Profile.
- 8 is the standard, not the finish.

Retired, never use: Power Follows Capability. Built to Handle More as the
flagship. THINK/BUILD/ADAPT/PERFORM/LEAD/CONNECT. Compass, in any form. Forged
By War.

## Anti-references

Explicit "must never look like" list from the visual identity lock:

- Generic black-and-red alpha-male brand.
- Faux Spartan, Viking, or warrior mythology. No shields, crowns, lions, wolves,
  Spartan helmets, swords, chess kings, flames, crosshairs, or secret-society
  insignia.
- Tactical or military cosplay.
- Luxury-flex influencer feed. No private jets, no designer logos as message.
- Sterile cybersecurity corporate website.
- Bodybuilding or fitness-only creator.
- Academic professor with no lived action.
- Motivational quote account.
- Overdesigned futurist or AI-neon aesthetic. No Matrix green, no RGB, no neon
  cyber gradients, no generic blue SaaS gradient.
- A résumé listing every job and certification above the fold.
- A homepage with six equal CTAs.
- A site that looks impressive but cannot capture email.

Also banned by the blueprint: bright gold luxury signalling, four-card framework
grids with no storytelling, fully-WebGL pages where text is inaccessible, and
any interaction the visitor must learn before they can navigate.

## Strategic Design Principles

1. **Clarity wins ties.** Spectacle is allowed. Every interaction must earn its
   place; if it only decorates, remove it.
2. **One dominant action per page.** Never give equal visual weight to
   newsletter, consultation, community, programs, and contact at once.
3. **The homepage establishes the worldview.** It does not sell every service.
   Dedicated pages convert once intent is known.
4. **Proof over proclamation.** Evidence accumulates through documented work and
   real relationships, not status claims.
5. **Non-averaging.** The product's core idea is that a strong dimension must
   never hide a weak one. The interface must never show a single overall score.
6. **Insight before extraction.** Meaningful results are shown before any email
   is requested.
7. **No fake science.** Never "validated", "diagnostic", "psychometric", or
   "percentile" unless real validation exists.
8. **Honest empty states.** Do not launch empty navigation categories dressed as
   full ones, and never fabricate proof, articles, testimonials, or imagery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Full keyboard navigation; visible focus states; screen-reader labels on all
  response controls.
- Never rely on colour alone for score bands, the standard line, or gap
  categories. Band names always render as text.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: non-essential scaling, panning, and large
  spatial movement reduce or disappear. Content is never gated behind motion.
- Result charts carry accessible text equivalents.
- Slider questions must also be answerable with discrete buttons or numeric
  entry.
- Critical text and navigation exist as real HTML, never only inside a canvas.
- Performance is part of accessibility: LCP ≤ 2.5s, INP ≤ 200ms, CLS ≤ 0.1.
- Mobile is a deliberately simplified experience that feels premium on its own,
  not a desktop page with effects stripped off.
